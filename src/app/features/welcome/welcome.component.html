Welcome to Café Parisius

Café Parisius is named after Arthur Parisius, the legendary Surinamese saxophonist who made a name for himself in Amsterdam during the 1930s. With live jazz, fine drinks, and an open-door atmosphere, Parisius fits right into a neighborhood where diverse cultures and stories have converged for over a century.

Who was Arthur Parisius?

Arthur Parisius was born in Suriname in 1911 and arrived in the Netherlands as a young man, having stowed away on a ship. In Amsterdam, he found his niche in the jazz club scene and became known as "Kid Dynamite"—one of the first major Surinamese jazz musicians in the Netherlands. He played swing and jazz but also incorporated Surinamese influences. His music gained popularity in clubs around Rembrandtplein and, later, on the Zeedijk. His story is one of talent, migration, and the struggle to carve out a place in Amsterdam.

AMSTERDAM & JAZZ

In the 1920s and 1930s, Amsterdam was captivated by jazz. Clubs sprang up around Rembrandtplein where American, European, and Surinamese musicians came together. Names such as Louis Armstrong, Duke Ellington, Teddy Cotton, and Kid Dynamite are part of that early history. For many Surinamese musicians, Amsterdam was a place of opportunity, yet also a city where they faced racism and exclusion. Precisely because of this, jazz became a unique meeting point between worlds.
